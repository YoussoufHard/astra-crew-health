## Walkthrough

For an in-app walkthrough, select **Guided demo** in the header. Seven steps show the scenario, explanations, trend, check-in, daily routine, report and NASA resources. The tour does not automatically save observations or change checklist progress. Exit restores your previous view and scenario.

1. Open Overview. Read the synthetic-data disclosure and inspect the six indicators.
2. Select **Sleep disruption**. Sleep changes to 4.8 hours, compared with a fictional baseline of 7.5 hours. Mood also changes.
3. Select the sleep card. Inspect the synthetic trend and the written demonstration rule.
4. Select a body system to read its educational context.
5. Open **Log health check**. Enter demonstration observations and save. This restores the routine scenario and displays the new entry.
6. Open **Daily protocol**. Check an action to see progress update.
7. Open **Health trends** and select **Export report**. The JSON downloads locally; no report is transmitted to a medical team.
8. Open **Science & sources** for the three NASA resources and the limits of the prototype.

## What the demonstration establishes

A working observation and documentation loop, with explicit rule explanations. It does not establish clinical effectiveness, sensor accuracy or operational readiness for a flight mission. Intended benefits have not yet been measured in a user study.

SI assisted development, writing and synthetic samples. NASA resources inform the educational framework. They do not supply the synthetic observations or validate the alert rules.
