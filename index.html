MEASURE WITH CONTEXT

A useful check-in starts here.

  1. Prepare the measurement.

    Use mission-approved equipment and its instructions. Record whether you were resting, exercising, or feeling unwell.

  2. Enter values and units.

    The demo accepts manual observations. It does not measure from a camera or connect to wearables.

  3. Review unusual changes.

    Verify the reading and consult the crew medical officer using mission procedures. A quiet dashboard does not rule out illness.

Sources & transparency

Prototype boundaries

All starting readings and mission details are synthetic. Manual entries stay in this browser's local storage, which is not encrypted by this application. No data is sent to a medical team. No diagnosis, prescription, validated risk score, or real-time radiation assessment is provided.

SI disclosure: OpenAI Codex assisted planning, writing, code, and synthetic demonstration data. No NASA logos, flags, mission identifiers, or NASA design assets are used. This independent project is not endorsed by NASA.
